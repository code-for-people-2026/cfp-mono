# Public Website

The public website explains who Code for People is, makes its commitments visible, and connects visitors to the products it is currently building.

## Language

**码成仝**:
The organization and parent brand under which public commitments and products are presented.

**近邻互助组**:
码成仝's current and only flagship product, helping neighbors find one another through real firsthand experience and complete concrete acts of mutual aid.
